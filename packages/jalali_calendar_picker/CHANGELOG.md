## 0.1.0

* First release, published as `jalali_calendar_picker` because pub.dev holds `jalali_date_picker` too close to the existing `jalalidatepicker`.

* `PersianCalendarDatePicker` and `PersianCalendarRangePicker` inline calendars with day, month, and year views.
* `showPersianDatePicker` and `showPersianDateRangePicker` dialogs.
* Saturday-first weeks, Persian digits, right-to-left layout, and limits from `jalali_date`.
